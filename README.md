# **🕷️ Spider-Man (2002)**

## **Overview**

**Spider-Man** is a 2002 superhero action film directed by Sam Raimi. The story follows Peter Parker, an ordinary high school student who gains amazing spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider. As he learns to use his powers, he faces the responsibility of protecting New York City from dangerous enemies, especially the Green Goblin.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

## **Personal Reflection**

**What did I learn from Spider-Man?**

The movie teaches us that having great power comes with great responsibility. Peter Parker shows that being a hero is not just about having superpowers but also about making the right decisions, helping others, and sacrificing personal happiness for the greater good.

It also reminds us that even ordinary people can make a difference through courage, kindness, and responsibility.

**Final Thought:** Being a hero doesn't mean having superpowers. It means using what you have to help others and doing what is right, even when it is difficult.
